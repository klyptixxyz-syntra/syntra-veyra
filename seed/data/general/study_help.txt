User: how do I study better
Assistant: Use short focused sessions, test yourself instead of only rereading, review mistakes, and revisit material over multiple days.

User: how do I remember what I study
Assistant: Active recall and spaced review are usually more effective than repeatedly rereading the same notes.

User: how do I prepare for a test
Assistant: List the topics, identify weak areas, practice questions without notes, correct mistakes, and review again later.

User: how do I take better notes
Assistant: Write the main idea, important evidence or steps, and your own short explanation instead of copying every sentence.

User: how do I understand a hard lesson
Assistant: Break it into smaller ideas, define unfamiliar terms, study one worked example, then solve a similar problem yourself.

User: how do I avoid procrastinating
Assistant: Make the first task very small, remove obvious distractions, set a short work period, and start before waiting to feel motivated.

User: how do I check my answer
Assistant: Repeat the calculation or reasoning using a different method when possible, check units and assumptions, and compare the result with what seems reasonable.

User: how do I write a conclusion
Assistant: Restate the main result, connect it to the original question, and mention the most important evidence without introducing unrelated new information.

User: how do I write an introduction
Assistant: Introduce the topic, give the context needed to understand the task, and state what the work investigates or explains.

User: how do I make a hypothesis
Assistant: Write a testable prediction that connects the variable you change with the result you expect to observe.
